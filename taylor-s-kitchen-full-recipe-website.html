Veggie Egg White Omelette

⏱ 15 minServes 1Breakfast
A fluffy, protein-packed egg white omelette stuffed with sautéed spinach, mushrooms, and bell peppers. Light, satisfying, and under 200 calories.
Your Rating:
★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$3.50

Ingredients

ItemCost
4 large egg whites$0.80
1/2 cup baby spinach$0.30
1/4 cup mushrooms, sliced$0.40
1/4 cup bell pepper, diced$0.35
2 tbsp onion, diced$0.10
1 tsp olive oil$0.10
Salt, pepper, herbs$0.05
1 tbsp feta cheese (opt)$0.40

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat. Sauté onion, bell pepper, and mushrooms 3–4 minutes until softened. Add spinach and cook 1 minute until wilted. Season and set aside.
  2. Whisk egg whites with a pinch of salt and pepper until slightly frothy.
  3. Wipe the pan clean and spray lightly with cooking spray over medium-low heat. Pour in egg whites and let set for 1–2 minutes.
  4. Spoon veggie filling onto one half of the omelette. Sprinkle with feta if using.
  5. Fold the omelette in half and slide onto a plate. Serve immediately.

★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$3.20

ItemCost

Instructions

ItemCost

Instructions

⏱ 30 minMakes 24Dessert
Nutty browned butter adds incredible depth to these thick, chewy chocolate chip cookies. The best cookies you'll ever make.
Your Rating:
★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$7.50 (~$0.31/cookie)

Ingredients

ItemCost
2 1/4 cups all-purpose flour$0.45
1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter$1.60
3/4 cup granulated + 3/4 cup brown sugar$0.60
2 large eggs$0.60
2 tsp vanilla extract$0.30
1 tsp baking soda + 1 tsp salt$0.10
2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips$3.00
Flaky sea salt for topping$0.20

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F. Line baking sheets with parchment.
  2. Brown butter in a saucepan over medium heat, stirring constantly, 5–7 minutes until golden and nutty. Cool 10 minutes.
  3. Whisk sugars into butter. Add eggs and vanilla; whisk vigorously until glossy.
  4. Stir in flour, baking soda, and salt until just combined. Fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Scoop rounded tablespoons onto sheets 2 inches apart. Bake 9–11 minutes until edges are golden. Sprinkle with sea salt. Cool 5 minutes before transferring.

No-Bake Strawberry Cheesecake

⏱ 20 min + chillServes 8Dessert
A buttery graham cracker crust with a luscious cream cheese filling and fresh strawberry topping. No oven required.
Your Rating:
★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$14.00 (~$1.75/serving)

Ingredients

ItemCost
2 cups graham cracker crumbs$1.50
6 tbsp butter, melted$0.90
2 blocks (8 oz ea) cream cheese$4.00
1/2 cup powdered sugar$0.30
1 tsp vanilla extract$0.15
1 cup heavy whipping cream$1.50
1 lb fresh strawberries$3.00
3 tbsp strawberry jam, warmed$0.50

Instructions

  1. Mix graham crumbs, melted butter, and 2 tbsp sugar. Press into a 9-inch springform pan. Refrigerate 15 minutes.
  2. Beat cream cheese, powdered sugar, and vanilla until smooth. Fold in whipped cream until just combined.
  3. Pour filling over crust. Refrigerate 4–6 hours or overnight until firmly set.
  4. Toss strawberries with warmed jam. Arrange on top of cheesecake. Release springform, slice, and serve.

Classic Apple Crisp

⏱ 50 minServes 8Dessert
Tender cinnamon-spiced apples under a buttery, crunchy oat topping. Serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream for pure fall comfort.
Your Rating:
★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$9.00 (~$1.13/serving)

Ingredients

ItemCost
6 medium apples, peeled & sliced$3.00
2 tbsp sugar + 1 tsp cinnamon$0.20
1 cup rolled oats$0.40
1/2 cup all-purpose flour$0.15
1/2 cup packed brown sugar$0.30
1/2 tsp cinnamon + 1/4 tsp nutmeg$0.10
1/4 tsp salt$0.02
6 tbsp cold butter, cubed$0.90

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Toss apple slices with sugar and 1 tsp cinnamon. Spread in a greased 9x13 baking dish.
  2. Mix oats, flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt. Work in cold butter with fingertips until mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  3. Sprinkle topping evenly over apples.
  4. Bake 35–40 minutes until topping is golden brown and apples are bubbling and tender.
  5. Serve warm with vanilla ice cream or whipped cream.

Fudgy Scratch Brownies

⏱ 35 minMakes 16Dessert
Dense, intensely chocolatey brownies with a crinkle top and fudgy center. Made entirely from scratch with pantry staples.
Your Rating:
★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$6.50 (~$0.41/brownie)

Ingredients

ItemCost
1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter$0.80
1 cup granulated sugar$0.25
2 large eggs$0.60
1 tsp vanilla extract$0.15
1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder$0.50
1/2 cup all-purpose flour$0.15
1/4 tsp salt + 1/4 tsp baking powder$0.05
1/2 cup chocolate chips (opt)$0.75

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease an 8x8 inch baking pan and line with parchment.
  2. Melt butter in a saucepan over low heat or in the microwave. Remove from heat and stir in sugar.
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla. Stir in cocoa powder, flour, salt, and baking powder until just combined.
  4. Fold in chocolate chips if using. Pour into prepared pan and spread evenly.
  5. Bake 25–28 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  6. Cool completely in pan before cutting into 16 squares.

Classic Lemon Bars

⏱ 1 hrMakes 16Dessert
A buttery shortbread crust under a bright, tangy, perfectly set lemon curd filling. Dusted with powdered sugar and irresistible.
Your Rating:
★★★★★
Estimated Meal Cost: ~$6.00 (~$0.38/bar)

Ingredients

ItemCost
1 cup all-purpose flour$0.20
1/2 cup butter, softened$0.80
1/4 cup powdered sugar (crust)$0.15
2 large eggs$0.60
1 cup granulated sugar (filling)$0.25
2 tbsp all-purpose flour (filling)$0.05
Juice + zest of 2 lemons$1.00
Powdered sugar for dusting$0.15

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Mix flour, butter, and 1/4 cup powdered sugar until it forms a dough. Press into greased 8x8 pan. Bake 15–18 minutes until lightly golden.
  2. Meanwhile, whisk together eggs, granulated sugar, 2 tbsp flour, lemon juice, and zest until smooth.
  3. Pour lemon filling over the hot crust and return to the oven.
  4. Bake 20–25 minutes until filling is set (no jiggle).
  5. Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 1 hour. Dust with powdered sugar and cut into 16 bars.
